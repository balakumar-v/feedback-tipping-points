Randomised slice CORE

round 1 uniform (propensity 1/10), later rounds repeat the last item w.p. 0.5 (propensity known), 10 rounds (last-only fails: κ-hat bias −0.27…−0.33). Refits base, κ-hat and static lift. A 3,000-user pilot only sizes n*; the final verdict uses fresh users. Cost: uniform serving lost 24% of clicks on OBD (Random 0.51% vs BTS 0.67%), so slices are capped at 8,000 users.
κ-hat is population-averaged: hidden taste attenuates it (−0.18 at taste ×2), but κ* is solved in the same model, so the forecast is compared like-for-like.

Verdict rule (frozen before held-out runs)

All intervals: two-sided 95% joint user-bootstrap. APPROVE iff static-lift and forecast-lift lower ends > 0 for all four shapes and a count-curve model, and a coverage guard: at most 5% of the new policy's forecast showings may have prior counts the slice saw under 100 times.
REJECT iff, for all four, the static or forecast upper end ≤ 0.
NEED DATA otherwise (no slice: REJECT or NEED DATA only); asks for a fresh slice of n* users: n* = ((1.96 + 0.84) s / |estimate|)² per shape (95% interval, 80% power), max over shapes, s = bootstrap SD × √(pilot size), pilot = 3,000 users. Above 8,000 users it abstains and reports n* with a pilot band.
